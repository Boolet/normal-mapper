Selection

Click a vertex or pixel to select it, Shift-click to toggle, Shift-drag to box-select, Alt-drag to deselect. You can also drag across pixels in the texture preview.
Use the transform tool to edit: drag the dot to move the pivot (Alt skips snapping), the red/blue rings to tilt, and the arrows to raise or lower the selection. Del flattens it to 0.
